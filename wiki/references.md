# External References (Link-Only)

External resources that the wiki links to for lookup and cross-referencing, but which are **not** registered raw-source collections — they are not fetched, snapshotted, or ingested by the nightly pipeline. Accepted proposals that were designated "link-only" land here.

## Indexes and Community Resources

- **Virtual Pinball Spreadsheet** — <https://virtualpinballspreadsheet.github.io/>
  Community index of VPX table downloads, ROM names, and patch links; used by community wikis (e.g. the Unofficial Visual Pinball Wiki) as the authority for locating parent/clone ROM sets. Linked from table-setup and ROM-related pages as a lookup aid.

## Conventions

- Entries are added via accepted source proposals designated "link-only", or by direct request.
- A link-only entry is never fetched into `raw/` and never gets a `wiki/sources/` page.
- Wiki pages may link to these resources as external lookup aids; factual claims still require Primary/Secondary tier citations per AGENTS.md.
